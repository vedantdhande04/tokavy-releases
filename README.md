## WHAT IT DOES

Tokavy runs silently in your system tray. Select any prompt in any app, press a keyboard shortcut, and your text is instantly refined — shorter, clearer, or better engineered — then pasted back in place.

No copy-paste. No context switching. No tab switching. Just better prompts.

---

## HOW IT WORKS

**STEP 01 — Select your prompt**  
Highlight any prompt in your favorite AI app.

**STEP 02 — Press your shortcut**  
Use Compress or Enhance with a single keystroke.

**STEP 03 — Get your refined prompt**  
Your optimized prompt replaces the original instantly.

---

## SHORTCUTS

| Shortcut | Mode | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Ctrl + Alt + J` | **Compress** | Strips filler, cuts tokens, keeps full intent. |
| `Ctrl + Alt + K` | **Enhance** | Restructures into a prompt-engineered format for better output. |

Shortcuts are fully customizable from the settings panel.
